The AIS Research Group is affiliated with the College of Computer Science and Technology at Taiyuan University of Technology and conducts research with the support of the Shanxi Key Laboratory of Industrial Internet Security.

Our research focuses on industrial Internet security, artificial intelligence security, cybersecurity, data security, and privacy protection. We are committed to addressing critical security challenges arising from the integration of intelligent, networked, and industrial systems.

## Research Areas

Our primary research areas include industrial Internet security, artificial intelligence security, cybersecurity, data and privacy protection, intelligent intrusion detection, and cybersecurity situational awareness.

### About Us

The AIS Research Group combines frontier scientific research with practical application requirements. Our work covers theoretical research, algorithm design, system implementation, and engineering applications.

The group includes faculty members, graduate students, and undergraduate students. We welcome students interested in industrial Internet security, artificial intelligence security, cybersecurity, and data security, as well as academic and industrial collaborations.
