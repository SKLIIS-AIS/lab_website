## Research Areas

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/Industrial-Internet-Security.jpg" class="research-thumb" alt="Industrial Internet Security">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Industrial Internet Security</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We conduct research on security challenges in the industrial Internet, intelligent manufacturing, and critical information infrastructure. Our work focuses on industrial control system security, industrial protocol security, anomalous behavior detection, edge device security, and industrial network attack prevention, with the goal of improving the security, reliability, and controllability of industrial Internet systems.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/AI-Safety.jpg" class="research-thumb" alt="Artificial Intelligence Security">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Artificial Intelligence Security</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We study security risks arising throughout the training, deployment, and application of artificial intelligence models. Our research includes adversarial attacks and defenses, model robustness evaluation, trustworthy artificial intelligence, privacy-preserving machine learning, and large language model security, promoting the safe and reliable use of AI systems in complex environments.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/Cybersecurity.jpg" class="research-thumb" alt="Cybersecurity">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Cybersecurity</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Our research focuses on cyberattack detection, malicious traffic identification, vulnerability analysis, threat intelligence, cybersecurity situational awareness, and offensive and defensive security. By combining machine learning, data mining, and system security technologies, we aim to improve the detection, analysis, and response capabilities for sophisticated cyberattacks.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/Data-Security-and-Privacy-Protection.jpg" class="research-thumb" alt="Data Security and Privacy Protection">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Data Security and Privacy Protection</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We investigate security and privacy challenges throughout data collection, transmission, storage, sharing, and intelligent analysis. Our research covers data security governance, privacy-preserving computation, federated learning security, data leakage detection, access control, and sensitive information protection, providing security guarantees for data-driven intelligent systems.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/Intrusion-Detection-and-Anomaly-Behavior-Analysis.jpg" class="research-thumb" alt="Intrusion Detection and Anomalous Behavior Analysis">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Intrusion Detection and Anomalous Behavior Analysis</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We study anomalous behaviors and malicious activities in network systems, industrial systems, and intelligent devices. Our work explores intrusion detection methods based on traffic analysis, log analysis, behavior modeling, and deep learning, with the goal of developing efficient, accurate, and interpretable security detection and early-warning mechanisms.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/Security-Assessment-and Trusted-Protection.jpg" class="research-thumb" alt="Security Assessment and Trusted Protection">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Security Assessment and Trusted Protection</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We conduct research on security risk assessment for systems, networks, algorithms, and applications. Our work focuses on security testing, vulnerability discovery, risk quantification, attack and defense validation, trusted computing, and the construction of comprehensive security protection frameworks, providing systematic assessment and protection solutions for industrial Internet and intelligent systems.</p>
</div>
</div>
